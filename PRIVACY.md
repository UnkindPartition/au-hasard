*Au hasard* is a random number generator and list picker for Android, developed by
Roman Cheplyaka.

## Summary

*Au hasard* does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything stays on your
device.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising,
  or crash-reporting code.
- **No network access.** The app does not request the Internet permission and
  cannot connect to any server. Links in the About page open in your web
  browser, which is not part of the app.
- **No permissions.** The app does not request any Android permissions.
- **Local storage only.** Lists you save and your app settings are stored only
  on your device, in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android
  cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. Uninstalling the app deletes
  them.
- **Clipboard.** The Paste button reads the clipboard only when you tap it; the
  Copy button writes to it.
- **Randomness.** Random numbers are generated on your device using the
  operating system's cryptographically secure random number generator,
  combined with the timing of your taps.

## App stores

If you install *Au hasard* from an app store such as Google Play or F-Droid, the
store may collect data according to its own privacy policy. The developer does
not receive personal data from these stores beyond what they provide to all
developers in aggregated, anonymized form (for example, install counts).

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page, with an updated date
